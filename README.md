# My Favorite Horror Movies

## Introduction
I enjoy watching horror movies, especially those involving supernatural events, mysterious rituals, curses, and paranormal activities.

## My Favorite Movie: Incantation

**Incantation (2022)** is one of my favorite horror movies because of its creepy atmosphere, disturbing scenes, and interesting storyline. I like how the movie involves mysterious rituals and supernatural elements.

## Other Horror Movies I Recommend

Here are some horror movies similar to Incantation:

1. **The Medium (2021)** - A Thai supernatural horror movie about shamanism and possession.
2. **The Wailing (2016)** - A Korean horror movie involving mysterious events and evil spirits.
3. **Satan's Slaves (2017)** - An Indonesian horror movie about a family experiencing terrifying supernatural events.
4. **Gonjiam: Haunted Asylum (2018)** - A Korean found-footage horror movie set in an abandoned asylum.
5. **Noroi: The Curse (2005)** - A Japanese horror movie about a mysterious curse.
